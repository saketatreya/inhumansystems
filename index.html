02 / research

The machines have some explaining to do

Papers & supporting evidence →
  1. 01

    What does rollout error actually measure?

    Auditing released navigation world models from 49M to 1.01B parameters. Correcting rollout commands reduced the 1B model's 4 FPS LPIPS by 18.8% on the confirmation cohort, without retraining.

    World models · evaluation · 2026 manuscript

  2. 02

    Compute what the camera determines. Learn the remainder.

    Analytic camera-motion transport in frozen vision encoders: a rotation warp with no fitted parameters explains 16–37% of held-out feature change. Studying where geometry helps, and where pooling hides it.

    Representation learning · geometry · workshop manuscript

  3. 03

    Reading relatedness and hierarchy

    Across five modalities, low exact split recovery can coexist with substantial local hierarchical agreement. Measuring what a declared readout makes accessible, including the effects of centering and normalization.

    Self-supervised learning · hierarchy · revised manuscript
